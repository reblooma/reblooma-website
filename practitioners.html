Questions therapists ask.

What is an FSA or HSA?

Money set aside from a paycheck before taxes, for health care. Over 60 million Americans hold one. FSA funds expire each year, and nearly half of holders lose some.

Does massage qualify?

More often than you'd think. Chronic shoulder and back pain, headaches, sleep problems, stress-related tension: you're almost certainly treating these already. Relaxation alone doesn't qualify, but a documented condition does. The setting is never the test. The records are.

I work at a day spa, a resort, or I'm mobile. Can I join?

Yes. Day spas, resorts, wellness practices and mobile or in-home therapists are all welcome. You need to be a licensed massage therapist, and the session has to treat what your client's letter covers.

If your spa takes payment rather than you, we set things up with them so the invoice comes from the business that was paid.

I'm not clinically trained. Can I still write the notes?

Yes. You answer a few plain prompts: what your client told you, where you worked, what changed. Reblooma turns them into a session note for you to check and sign.

You never diagnose. The condition comes from the clinician's letter. If a session drifts outside what the letter covers, you see it before you sign.

My card machine already takes FSA/HSA cards. Do I need this?

A swipe clearing means the card wasn't declined. It doesn't mean the visit qualifies. If the administrator later asks for proof and there is none, your patient repays it. That's the patient who quietly stops booking.

When do I get paid?

Once you sign the note and the invoice is made. You never wait on an insurer, and your pay never depends on your patient's claim.

What does it cost me?

The platform is free: no signup fee, nothing per session. One optional add-on, an AI seat that drafts your notes, is priced before launch. Card processing fees come out of payouts, as with any terminal.

Who approves the claim?

Your patient's plan administrator decides, not you and not us. So the claim is never yours to chase. Reblooma makes sure the documents are ready before anyone asks, and if the administrator wants more, we help your patient answer.4
