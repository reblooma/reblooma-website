About Reblooma

Massage is care.
The paperwork should say so.

Millions of people have money set aside for health care that could pay for the massage they already need. Almost none of it gets used that way, because no massage practice was set up to produce the documents. We started Reblooma to fix that part.

Why we started

I built Reblooma from my own search for relief.

After becoming a mother, I developed chronic pain while working long hours. Massage helped me get back on my feet. I wanted to keep it part of my recovery, without wondering whether I had the right records to support FSA or HSA spending.

I’ve spent my career in audit, compliance, and finance systems, so I know how much those details matter. An independent clinical evaluation, a practitioner-signed treatment note, and an itemized invoice each tell part of the story. Reblooma brings them together so patients can pursue eligible reimbursement with documentation in hand, while local practices spend less time gathering paperwork.

I built Reblooma for people trying to feel better while managing everything else life asks of them, and for the local practitioners who care for them. My hope is simple: make it easier to seek the care that helps, with a little more clarity and peace of mind along the way.

Joyce W.Founder, Reblooma

What we believe

Three ideas that shape every decision we make.

The setting isn't the test.

A day spa, a clinic, or someone who comes to your home. What qualifies care is the treatment and the documentation, not the room it happened in.

Practitioners shouldn't discount.

Patients pay less because their tax rate does the work. The practitioner's rate stays where they set it.

Paperwork should prove something.

A letter that nobody checks against the care proves nothing. The note and invoice show, session by session, that the treatment matched the letter.

Who does what

We're clear about what we do, and what we don't.

Health care records only earn trust when every step has an accountable owner. Here is who owns each one.

Reblooma

Builds the software. Assembles your notes, invoices and letter into one file. Runs the practice tools.

Dr. B

An independent telehealth practice. Its clinicians review each request and decide whether to issue a letter.

Your practitioner

Does the treatment, then records what they worked on and signs the clinical note.

Your plan administrator

Reviews the claim and decides reimbursement. If they ask for more, we help you answer it.
