ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT


This statement applies to the https://reblooma.com website.

OUR COMMITMENT

Reblooma is committed to ensuring our services are accessible to people with disabilities. We aim to conform with the WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This accessibility statement forms an integral part of our Terms of Use: https://reblooma.com/terms.html.

ACCESSIBILITY FEATURES

You should be able to:
  • Zoom in up to 200% without loss of information or functionality
  • Navigate the website with just a keyboard
  • Listen to the website using a screen reader

CONFORMANCE STATUS

Status: Partially compliant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA

We conducted our most recent accessibility evaluation in 11/11/2026. For more information, see the HOW WE TEST and NON-ACCESSIBLE CONTENT section.

NON-ACCESSIBLE CONTENT

Below is a description of known limitations we're working to address.

1. Some text is too light: Some hints and small notes do not have enough contrast with the background. We are making them darker.

2. Uploaded letters: A letter you upload is a photo or a PDF from your clinician, and a screen reader may not be able to read it. What we read from the letter is shown as normal text on the next screen.

3. Card entry: When you pay through Reblooma, the card form comes from our payment processor. We rely on their accessibility work for that part.

We are actively working to address these issues as part of an ongoing plan to improve accessibility.

PREPARATION OF THIS STATEMENT

This statement was prepared on 11 November 2026 and is reviewed annually or when we make significant service changes.

We maintain records of accessibility feedback and our responses for regulatory compliance.

HOW WE TEST

We evaluate accessibility using:
  • Automated scans with accessibility testing tools, plus a review by hand of keyboard use, labels and color contrast

FEEDBACK AND CONTACT INFORMATION

Please let us know if you experience accessibility barriers using our services or need information in an alternative format.
  • Email: support@reblooma.com (mark subject line "Accessibility")
We respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the enforcement authority in your country:
  • Germany: BFIT-Bund
